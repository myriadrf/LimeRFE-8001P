Performance and Evaluation
##########################

This chapter gives ACPR (adjacent channel power ratio) measurement results for two external power amplifiers, driven and linearized by the LimeRFE 8001P board:

* Qorvo QPA4501 – a GaN power amplifier with the second stage in Doherty configuration. It delivers 3 W of linear output power in the 4.4 – 5.0 GHz frequency range.
* Skyworks SKY66520-11 – a two-stage 3.3 – 3.8 GHz power amplifier, which delivers 1 W of linear output power at ACPR < –50 dBc. It has a wide instantaneous bandwidth of 200 MHz.

In both cases the board is set up in the same way as in the :doc:`LTE example </user/examples/lte>`, with the main difference being the use of an external power amplifier.

QPA4501
=======

The ACPR measurement setup with linearization is shown in Figure 1 (block diagram) and Figure 2 (photo). It consists of:

* a signal generator, capable of generating a 20 MHz TM3.1 LTE signal,
* the LimeRFE 8001P board, with the hardware in the default configuration (see :ref:`orx-reconfiguration`) and the software configured as described in :doc:`/user/software/setup`,
* the external power amplifier QPA4501 (device under test),
* an external coupler (a Wilkinson power divider is used),
* a spectrum analyzer.

.. figure:: /images/LimeRFE-8001P_perf_qpa4501_setup.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 1: ACPR measurement setup with QPA4501 – block diagram

.. figure:: /images/LimeRFE-8001P_perf_qpa4501_photo.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 2: ACPR measurement setup with QPA4501 – photo

The IF frequency (signal generator) is set to 2 GHz and the RF frequency (spectrum analyzer) to 4.5 GHz. The LO frequency is 6.5 GHz (lower sideband at the mixer output).
The input signal is a standard 20 MHz TM3.1 LTE signal. The measurement results are given in Table 1. Spectrum analyzer screenshots with linearization OFF and ON are shown in Figure 3 and Figure 4.

SKY66520-11
===========

The measurement setup with the SKY66520-11 power amplifier is shown in Figure 5.
The IF frequency (signal generator) is set to 2 GHz and the RF frequency (spectrum analyzer) to 3.6 GHz. The LO frequency is 5.6 GHz (lower sideband at the mixer output).
The input signals are standard TM3.1 signals with different bandwidths, generated by an AMD RFSoC evaluation board.

  :width: 600

  Figure 5: ACPR measurement setup with SKY66520-11 – block diagram

Figure 8 shows the maximum output power with ACPR better than –45 dBc, for different signal bandwidths. The output power falls sharply for signal bandwidths greater than 80 MHz.

.. figure:: /images/LimeRFE-8001P_perf_sky66520_pout_bw.png
  :width: 600

  Figure 8: Maximum Pout vs. channel bandwidth (ACPR < –45 dBc)

Figure 9 shows ACPR low vs. output power of the SKY66520-11 without linearization, for a 20 MHz signal.
There is a "plateau" in the ACPR vs. output power curve of this amplifier. This is the reason for the sharp drop in the achievable linear output power at 90 MHz signal bandwidth in Figure 8.

.. figure:: /images/LimeRFE-8001P_perf_sky66520_acpr_pout.png
  :width: 600

  :width: 600

The results show that linearization works for all 5G NR channel bandwidths. However, the ACPR improvement decreases as the bandwidth increases, and so does the maximum linear output power.
